## 📌 Project Overview
This repository hosts a comprehensive Business Intelligence project analyzing transactional datasets for **ShopNest**, a Portuguese e-commerce marketplace. Developed as part of the Skillovilla curriculum, this project focuses on isolating supply chain delivery delays and regional performance gaps across different markets.

## 🎯 Business Metrics & Objectives
* Track financial performance across **\$13.59M in total historical sales**.
* Isolate regional bottlenecks causing logistics and shipping delays.
* Analyze product categories to maximize operational efficiency.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Validated
* **Primary Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Language:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* **Methodologies:** Data Modeling, ETL Architecture, Interactive Dashboard Design

## 💡 Engineering Implementation (What I Did)
* **Dashboard Engineering:** Built an interactive performance report structured across dedicated financial, operational, and product tabs.
* **Advanced DAX Queries:** Authored custom DAX queries to isolate payment method distributions, calculate rolling average product review scores, and count delayed shipments using delivery timestamps.

## 📈 Major Insights & Business Impact
* **Regional Drivers:** Identified that the **SP state** generated the highest sales volume, contributing **\$5.2M** to the marketplace.
* **Operational Bottlenecks:** Discovered that the `bed_bath_table` category logged the highest delay count with **4.3K orders** facing fulfillment issues.
* **Product Performance:** Verified that `health_beauty` led all categories in individual product sales volume at **\$1.3M**.
* **Financial Trends:** Found that **credit cards** dominated consumer payment types, accounting for **75.24%** of all transactions.
